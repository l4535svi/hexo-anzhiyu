首先点击shopify的apps,搜索框然后点击Apps and sales channels

![](https://sdaliblog.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/image-1.png)

点击上面的Develop apps-Allow Custom App developmen-Create an app

![](https://sdaliblog.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/image-2.png)

给app命名然后点名Create app,搜索和勾选以下选项write_products, read_products, write_themes, read_themes,然后点击Save保存。

![](https://sdaliblog.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/image-4.png)

点击右上角的”Install app” 按钮. 点击 跳出的”Install”安装按钮

![](https://sdaliblog.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/image-5.png)

这时候就会跳出一串API密码，点击 “Reveal token once”去获取API 访问令牌,有了这个访问令牌就可以到[https://pagepilot.ai/](https://pagepilot.ai/?utm_source=rw&via=dali0425)后台绑定了。

![](https://sdaliblog.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/image-6.png)

如何在PagePilot.ai当做绑定你的Shopify店铺

1\. Integrations -> Shopify -> 点击 “Connect”按钮

2\. 在网站的 “Website URL” 部分,输入你的shopify店铺链接

（店铺链接可以到你的shopify-Set-Domains）

![](https://sdaliblog.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/image-7.png)

粘贴API访问令牌然后点击”Connect Store”连接店铺，到这里就已经把落地页的工具和你的shopify绑定，之后就可以直接推送到你的shopify店铺。

![](https://sdaliblog.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/image-9.png)
